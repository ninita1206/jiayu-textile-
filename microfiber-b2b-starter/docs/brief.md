# Website brief — draft

## Markets

- Primary: United States
- Primary: Italy

## Buyers

- Footwear and bag designers
- Material development teams
- Purchasing teams

- Quality and compliance teams

## Application strategy

### Established

- Footwear
- Bags

- Lifestyle accessories

## Proposed positioning

Custom-developed microfiber materials for brands and manufacturers—from color, texture and performance development to testing and production.

This is draft positioning, not an approved company claim.

## Primary website conversions

1. Request material samples
2. Submit a material-development brief
3. Request a quotation
4. Request product-specific compliance documents

## Content principle

The website must distinguish current production-proven experience from growth areas and unvalidated new applications.
